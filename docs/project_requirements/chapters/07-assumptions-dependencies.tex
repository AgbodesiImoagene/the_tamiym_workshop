\section{Assumptions and Dependencies}

This section outlines the assumptions and external dependencies that underpin the
successful delivery of the project. These assumptions are intended to clarify
responsibilities, reduce ambiguity, and ensure alignment between stakeholders
throughout the development process.

\subsection{Design and Specification Assumptions}

The project is based on client-provided design specifications, including layouts,
user flows, and interaction patterns defined in Figma.

The following assumptions apply:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item The provided designs represent the authoritative reference for visual
    layout, user experience, and functional intent
    \item The implementation will aim to achieve functional and visual parity
    with the approved designs
    \item Any material changes to designs after development has commenced may
    require reassessment of scope, timeline, or cost
\end{itemize}

Minor design clarifications or adjustments required for technical feasibility or
usability may be proposed during implementation, subject to client approval.

\subsection{Content and Data Assumptions}

The following assumptions apply to platform content and operational data:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Product information, imagery, descriptions, and pricing inputs will be
    provided or approved by the client
    \item Fundraising campaign content (titles, descriptions, imagery, videos)
    is the responsibility of campaign organizers
    \item The platform is not responsible for the accuracy or legality of
    user-provided content beyond basic moderation controls
\end{itemize}

The quality and completeness of provided content may affect timelines and final
presentation.

\subsection{Third-Party Services and Integrations}

The platform relies on third-party services for certain functionality.

Assumptions include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Payment processing will be handled by third-party providers appropriate
    to the target market and agreed business requirements
    \item Availability, performance, and compliance of third-party services are
    outside the direct control of the platform
    \item Changes to third-party service terms, APIs, or availability may require
    system updates or adjustments
\end{itemize}

Any additional third-party integrations beyond those implied by the designs will
be treated as out-of-scope unless explicitly agreed.

\subsection{Operational and Administrative Assumptions}

The following operational assumptions apply:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Administrative users will be responsible for day-to-day platform
    operations, including order management, product maintenance, and moderation
    activities
    \item Platform policies governing acceptable content, refunds, cancellations,
    and fundraising compliance will be defined by the client
    \item The platform will provide tools to support administration but will not
    replace operational decision-making or customer support processes
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Legal and Compliance Assumptions}

The platform is intended to operate as a software system and does not provide
legal, financial, or regulatory advice.

Assumptions include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item The client is responsible for ensuring compliance with applicable laws,
    regulations, and tax requirements in the jurisdictions where the platform
    operates
    \item Fundraising activities conducted through the platform must comply with
    local fundraising, charitable, and consumer protection regulations
    \item The platform will support compliance through configuration and data
    capture where applicable but does not enforce jurisdiction-specific rules
    beyond agreed requirements
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Timeline and Collaboration Assumptions}

Successful delivery of the project depends on timely collaboration between
stakeholders.

Assumptions include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Client feedback and approvals will be provided within agreed review
    windows
    \item Delays in approvals, content delivery, or clarification may impact
    project timelines
    \item Ongoing communication will be maintained to address questions, risks,
    and clarifications as they arise
\end{itemize}
