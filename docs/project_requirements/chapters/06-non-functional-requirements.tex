\section{Non-Functional Requirements}

This section describes the non-functional requirements of the platform. These
requirements define the quality attributes, performance expectations, and
operational characteristics of the system, independent of specific feature
behavior or technical implementation details.

\subsection{Usability and User Experience}

The platform shall provide a clear, intuitive, and consistent user experience
across all supported areas of the system.

Key usability requirements include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Consistent visual styling and interaction patterns across public pages,
    customer dashboards, fundraising views, and administrative interfaces
    \item Clear navigation and page hierarchy that enables users to locate features
    and information with minimal effort
    \item Responsive layouts that adapt to common desktop, tablet, and mobile
    screen sizes, in alignment with the approved designs
    \item Clear feedback for user actions such as form submissions, saves,
    successful purchases, and error conditions
\end{itemize}

The Design Workshop experience shall prioritize clarity and responsiveness to
ensure users can confidently preview and adjust designs before purchase.

\subsection{Performance and Responsiveness}

The platform shall be designed to deliver acceptable performance under expected
usage conditions.

Performance requirements include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Page loads and primary interactions should complete within reasonable
    timeframes under normal operating conditions
    \item Interactive features such as product browsing and the Design Workshop
    should remain responsive during typical user workflows
    \item Administrative dashboards and reporting views should load efficiently,
    even as data volumes grow over time
\end{itemize}

The system is not required to support real-time or sub-second guarantees but
should provide a smooth and reliable user experience suitable for commercial use.

\subsection{Security and Data Protection}

The platform shall implement appropriate safeguards to protect user data and
system integrity.

Security-related requirements include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Secure authentication and account access for registered users and
    administrative users
    \item Protection of sensitive information such as account credentials and
    payment-related data
    \item Logical separation between customer-accessible functionality and
    administrative capabilities
    \item Measures to prevent unauthorized access, misuse, or data leakage
\end{itemize}

Payment processing shall rely on trusted third-party providers where applicable,
and the platform shall not store sensitive payment details beyond what is
necessary for operational purposes.

\subsection{Reliability and Availability}

The platform shall be designed to operate reliably under normal usage conditions.

Reliability requirements include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Stable operation during typical user activity, including browsing,
    customization, checkout, and order management
    \item Graceful handling of errors, including informative messaging where
    appropriate
    \item Preservation of user data such as saved designs, orders, and campaign
    configurations
\end{itemize}

The system is expected to be available during standard operating hours, subject
to planned maintenance and external service dependencies.

\subsection{Scalability and Growth Considerations}

The platform shall be designed with future growth in mind.

Scalability considerations include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Ability to support increasing numbers of users, products, orders, and
    fundraising campaigns over time
    \item Ability to extend reporting and analytics capabilities beyond the basic
    out-of-the-box features defined in this document
    \item Ability to introduce additional product types, customization options,
    and administrative workflows in future phases
\end{itemize}

While advanced scalability solutions are not required as part of the initial
delivery, the system should avoid design decisions that unnecessarily limit
future expansion.

\subsection{Browser and Device Support}

The platform shall support modern web browsers and devices.

Support requirements include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Compatibility with current versions of major desktop browsers
    (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
    \item Functional support for modern mobile browsers through responsive web
    design
    \item No requirement for legacy browser support unless explicitly agreed
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Maintainability and Extensibility}

The platform shall be structured to support ongoing maintenance and future
enhancements.

Maintainability requirements include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Clear separation between user-facing functionality and administrative
    tools
    \item Ability to update content, products, and campaigns without requiring
    code changes
    \item Ability to introduce new features and refinements in a controlled and
    predictable manner
\end{itemize}
