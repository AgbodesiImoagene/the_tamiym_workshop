\section{Project Overview}

The project involves the development of a complete, out-of-the-box (OOTB) web
platform for custom apparel ordering, product personalization, and fundraising.
The platform is designed to serve both individual customers and organizations
seeking to create branded merchandise for personal use, events, or fundraising
initiatives.

At a high level, the platform consists of:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Public Website} --- a marketing-facing site presenting the brand,
    product offerings, and fundraising value proposition.
    \item \textbf{Customer Dashboard} --- an authenticated area where users can browse
    products, create and manage designs, place orders, track order status, and manage
    account settings.
    \item \textbf{Design Workshop} --- an interactive customization experience that enables
    users to apply text and artwork to supported products, select product colors and sizes,
    and preview designs prior to purchase.
    \item \textbf{Checkout and Orders} --- a purchase flow for shipping information collection,
    payment processing (where applicable), order confirmation, and post-purchase order tracking.
    \item \textbf{Fundraising Module} --- tools for organizers to create fundraising campaigns,
    configure campaign settings, share campaign links, and track campaign performance.
    \item \textbf{Admin Dashboard} --- an internal interface for product, order, inventory, and
    campaign administration, as well as content moderation and platform oversight.
\end{itemize}

The platform will be implemented based on client-provided design specifications
(Figma), which define the core layout, interaction patterns, and user experience.
The implementation will prioritize functional accuracy, usability, and consistency
with the approved designs.

The success of the project will be measured by:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Accurate implementation of the approved design specifications across customer and admin experiences.
    \item A smooth and intuitive product browsing, customization, and checkout journey.
    \item Reliable handling of orders, fundraising campaigns, and operational workflows.
    \item A scalable foundation that supports future feature enhancements and reporting needs.
\end{itemize}
