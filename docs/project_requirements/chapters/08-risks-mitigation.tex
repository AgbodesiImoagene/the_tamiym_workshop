\section{Risks and Mitigation Strategies}

This section identifies key risks associated with the delivery and operation of
the platform and outlines mitigation strategies to reduce their potential impact.
The intent of this section is not to enumerate all possible risks, but to highlight
areas of complexity and demonstrate proactive planning.

\subsection{Design Workshop Complexity}

\textbf{Risk:}  
The interactive Design Workshop introduces complexity due to custom artwork
uploads, text placement, multiple product views (e.g., front/back/sleeve), and
real-time visual previews. User expectations around design accuracy may exceed
what is technically or operationally feasible for production.

\textbf{Mitigation:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Clearly communicate that on-screen previews are visual approximations
    of final printed output
    \item Constrain design placement to predefined printable areas
    \item Provide clear feedback and validation within the design workflow
    \item Align supported customization options strictly with the approved designs
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Fundraising Campaign Misuse or Compliance Issues}

\textbf{Risk:}  
Fundraising functionality may be misused or may introduce regulatory and
compliance concerns depending on jurisdiction.

\textbf{Mitigation:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Provide administrative tools to review, approve, disable, or moderate
    fundraising campaigns
    \item Require organizers to supply key campaign information and accept
    platform terms
    \item Clearly state that campaign organizers are responsible for compliance
    with applicable regulations
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Payment and Checkout Dependencies}

\textbf{Risk:}  
Reliance on third-party payment providers introduces dependencies related to
availability, transaction failures, and changes to external services.

\textbf{Mitigation:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Use established and widely adopted payment providers
    \item Provide clear user messaging in the event of payment failures
    \item Avoid storing sensitive payment information within the platform
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Operational and Administrative Overhead}

\textbf{Risk:}  
Without appropriate tooling, operational tasks such as order management,
inventory updates, and moderation could become resource-intensive.

\textbf{Mitigation:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Provide a centralized admin dashboard for core operational workflows
    \item Include search, filtering, and basic reporting to support day-to-day
    operations
    \item Ensure administrative interfaces follow the same usability principles
    as customer-facing views
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Scope Expansion and Feature Creep}

\textbf{Risk:}  
Additional feature requests or refinements beyond the agreed scope may arise
during development or after initial delivery.

\textbf{Mitigation:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Maintain this document as the authoritative reference for scope
    definition
    \item Evaluate new requests through a structured change review process
    \item Prioritize delivery of agreed core functionality before considering
    enhancements
\end{itemize}

\subsection{User Adoption and Usability Challenges}

\textbf{Risk:}  
Users may find certain workflows (e.g., customization or fundraising setup)
confusing or time-consuming, leading to reduced engagement.

\textbf{Mitigation:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Align implementation closely with the provided designs and interaction
    patterns
    \item Provide clear instructional text and contextual guidance where needed
    \item Use success states and progress indicators to reinforce completion
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data Growth and Reporting Limitations}

\textbf{Risk:}  
As platform usage grows, basic analytics and reporting views may become
insufficient for deeper business insights.

\textbf{Mitigation:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Limit initial analytics to clearly defined operational metrics
    \item Design reporting features to be extensible in future phases
    \item Clearly distinguish between basic out-of-the-box reporting and advanced
    analytics that may require additional effort
\end{itemize}
