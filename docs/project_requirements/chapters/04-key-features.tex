\section{Key Features and Capabilities}

This section outlines the primary features and capabilities of the platform from
a user and operational perspective. The descriptions focus on functional behavior
and expected outcomes rather than technical implementation details. All features
described below are aligned with the approved design specifications.

\subsection{Product Browsing and Discovery}

The platform provides users with a structured and intuitive product browsing
experience that allows them to explore available apparel items before initiating
customization or purchase.

Key capabilities include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Product listing views with visual previews and category-based navigation
    \item Individual product detail pages displaying images, descriptions, pricing
    information, and supported size and color options
    \item Presentation of related or recommended products to support discovery
    and upsell opportunities
\end{itemize}

The browsing experience is designed to be familiar and accessible, reducing
friction for first-time users while supporting efficient navigation for returning
customers.

\subsection{Design Workshop and Product Customization}

The Design Workshop is a core feature of the platform, enabling users to
personalize apparel items prior to purchase or fundraising use.

Capabilities include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Uploading custom artwork files for placement on supported products
    \item Adding and editing text elements, including positioning and basic styling
    \item Selecting product colors and supported size variants
    \item Switching between product views (e.g., front, back, sleeve) where applicable
    \item Real-time visual preview of designs within defined product areas
    \item Ability to save designs for later use and duplicate designs for iteration
\end{itemize}

The Design Workshop experience is intended to balance flexibility with simplicity,
providing clear visual feedback while maintaining consistency with production
constraints.

\subsection{Checkout and Order Management}

The platform supports a complete ordering flow that enables users to finalize
purchases efficiently and track order progress after checkout.

Key capabilities include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Collection of shipping and delivery information
    \item Selection of available payment methods as defined by the business
    \item Order summary review prior to confirmation
    \item Order success and confirmation states with clear next steps
    \item Order history and detailed order views within the user dashboard
    \item Visibility into order status progression (e.g., placed, processing, delivered)
\end{itemize}

This flow is designed to minimize abandonment while ensuring transparency
around costs, delivery expectations, and order status.

\subsection{Fundraising Campaign Management}

The fundraising module enables individuals and organizations to raise funds
through the sale of custom merchandise.

Core capabilities include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Creation of fundraising campaigns using a guided, multi-step workflow
    \item Selection and customization of campaign products via the Design Workshop
    \item Configuration of campaign details such as title, description, timeline,
    delivery method, and payout preferences
    \item Generation of customer-facing fundraiser pages with shareable links
    \item Support for campaign-specific pricing and profit estimation
    \item Basic tracking of campaign performance and progress toward goals
\end{itemize}

The fundraising experience is designed to be accessible to non-technical users,
allowing organizers to focus on storytelling, outreach, and engagement.

\subsection{Customer Account and Settings Management}

Registered users have access to account management tools that allow them to
maintain their personal information and preferences.

Capabilities include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Profile management (e.g., name, contact details, profile image)
    \item Password and authentication credential updates
    \item Management of saved shipping addresses
    \item Access to saved designs and past orders
\end{itemize}

These features ensure users can independently manage their accounts without
administrative intervention.

\subsection{Admin Dashboard and Operational Management}

The platform includes an administrative dashboard that enables internal users
to operate and maintain the system on an ongoing basis.

Admin capabilities include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Order administration, including viewing, searching, and updating order
    records and statuses
    \item Product and catalog management, including creation and maintenance
    of products, variants, and availability
    \item Inventory oversight, including visibility into stock levels and variant
    availability where applicable
    \item Fundraiser administration, including reviewing, approving, disabling,
    or assisting with campaign management
    \item User and content moderation tools for reviewing user-generated content
    and managing user access where required
\end{itemize}

The admin dashboard follows the same general style guide as the customer-facing
dashboard to ensure usability and consistency.

\subsection{Basic Analytics and Reporting}

To support day-to-day operational decision-making, the platform provides basic
analytics and reporting capabilities out-of-the-box.

These include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item High-level operational metrics such as order volume, revenue summaries,
    and campaign performance indicators
    \item Simple filtering options (e.g., date range, product, campaign)
    \item Export of selected reports in common formats (e.g., CSV) where applicable
\end{itemize}

These analytics are intended to provide visibility into platform activity without
introducing the complexity of advanced business intelligence systems.
