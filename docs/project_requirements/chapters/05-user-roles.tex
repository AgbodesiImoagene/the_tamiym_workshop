\section{User Roles and Access}

This section outlines the primary user roles supported by the platform and the
capabilities associated with each role. The purpose of this section is to clarify
responsibilities, access boundaries, and expected interactions with the system
from a functional perspective.

\subsection{Guest Users}

Guest users are visitors who access the platform without creating an account or
logging in.

Guest users are able to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Browse public website content, including marketing pages and product
    listings
    \item View individual product detail pages
    \item Access publicly available fundraising campaign pages via shared links
\end{itemize}

Guest users are not able to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Create or save product designs
    \item Place orders
    \item Create or manage fundraising campaigns
\end{itemize}

The guest experience is designed to encourage exploration while clearly guiding
users toward account creation when deeper interaction is required.

\subsection{Registered Customers}

Registered customers are users who have created an account and can access the
authenticated customer dashboard.

Registered customers are able to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Browse and view all available products
    \item Create, edit, save, duplicate, and manage custom designs
    \item Place orders for customized or standard products
    \item Complete checkout and payment flows where applicable
    \item View order history and track order status
    \item Manage personal profile information, passwords, and shipping details
\end{itemize}

Registered customers may also participate in fundraising campaigns by purchasing
campaign products through customer-facing fundraiser pages.

\subsection{Fundraiser Organizers}

Fundraiser organizers are registered users who create and manage fundraising
campaigns on the platform.

In addition to standard customer capabilities, fundraiser organizers are able to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Create fundraising campaigns using the guided campaign setup workflow
    \item Select and customize campaign products through the Design Workshop
    \item Configure campaign details such as campaign title, description, timeline,
    delivery method, and payout preferences
    \item Set pricing for campaign products and view estimated profit projections
    \item Access campaign-specific views showing basic performance and progress
\end{itemize}

Fundraiser organizers are responsible for the accuracy of campaign information
and for complying with any applicable policies defined by the platform.

\subsection{Administrative Users}

Administrative users are internal platform operators responsible for maintaining,
moderating, and overseeing the platform's operation.

Administrative users are able to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item View and manage customer orders, including updating order statuses
    \item Create, update, and manage product listings and categories
    \item Manage inventory-related information such as product variants and
    availability
    \item Review and administer fundraising campaigns, including approving,
    disabling, or assisting with campaign management
    \item Perform basic moderation actions on user-generated content and user
    accounts in accordance with platform policies
    \item Access basic analytics and reporting dashboards for operational oversight
\end{itemize}

Administrative access is restricted to authorized users and is intended solely
for platform operations and support purposes.

\subsection{Access Boundaries and Responsibilities}

Each user role is limited to the capabilities necessary to perform its intended
functions. The platform enforces clear separation between customer-facing and
administrative activities to ensure data integrity, security, and operational
control.

Role definitions and access levels may be refined over time as the platform
evolves, but any changes will preserve the core distinction between customers,
organizers, and administrators as defined in this document.
